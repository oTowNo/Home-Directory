🚶 Directions (1/3)

Step 1: Exit Woodlands MRT

From Woodlands MRT (NS line), head towards Exit 1 (Ave 3). Walk straight upon exiting.

Step 2: Walk Around Causeway Point

Walk around the outskirt of Causeway Point until you see a traffic road. Cross the road.

🚶 Directions (2/3)

Step 3: Walk on Left Pavement

Upon crossing, walk on the left pavement all the way until you see a T-junction.

Step 4: Cross the T-Junction

Once you see the T-junction road, cross the road.

🚶 Directions (3/3)

Step 5-6: Pass Through Block 361

Pass through Block 361 until you see a playground on the other side. Walk through the left pavement beside the playground until you see the road.

Step 7-8: Almost There!

Cross the road. You'll see a minimart Yeo Express at Block 354. Pass through the block - you'll see another playground and a court. Block 351 will be visible on the right side of the playground.

Final Step: You've Arrived! 🎉

Enter Block 351, take the lift to 7th floor, turn left to #07-733!
